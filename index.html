Yes. I can shape the look, build the website or app, and make sure the final result feels consistent.

We can keep updates simple through WhatsApp and email: clear checkpoints, shared feedback, and no disappearing act.

A short description of your idea, what you need it to do, and any websites or visuals you like is more than enough.
